# SIB Infotech - Comprehensive SEO Keyword Strategy

## Executive Summary

**Current State:** 280+ target keywords mapped. Most SEO service pages rank positions 40-60+ despite having dedicated pages. Main traffic comes from homepage (pos 10.69), career page (pos 4.08), and apply-now (pos 3.37). SEO pages are severely underperforming.

**Root Causes of Poor Rankings:**
1. Title tags are generic and not keyword-optimized for search intent
2. Pages lack sufficient depth (thin content vs competitors)
3. Cannibalization: multiple pages targeting overlapping keywords
4. Internal linking between SEO pages is weak
5. No programmatic location-based pages for "SEO company in [city]" keywords
6. Blog content doesn't target high-intent commercial queries

**Strategy:** Optimize existing pages with proper keyword mapping, create location pages, strengthen internal linking, and build topical authority through content clusters.
