### Section 1
- **Section Link Title**: Why Website Design Cost in India Is So Varied
- **Title**: Why Website Design Cost in India Is So Varied
- **Description**:
```
<p>Ask five web design agencies in India for a quote and you are likely to get five answers ranging from Rs. 10,000 to Rs. 10,00,000. That huge range exists because "website design" is not one product. It is a spectrum that runs from a simple brochure site built from a template to a fully custom platform with integrations, animations, and a dedicated design and development team.</p><p>The main variables that drive the price are:</p><ul><li><strong>Type of website:</strong> landing page, brochure site, e-commerce store, custom web application, or enterprise portal</li><li><strong>Number of pages:</strong> more pages mean more design work, more content, and more development time</li><li><strong>Feature complexity:</strong> payment gateways, booking systems, user logins, multilingual support, and third-party API integrations</li><li><strong>Design quality:</strong> template-based designs cost a fraction of custom UI/UX design</li><li><strong>Team experience:</strong> a reputed agency with senior designers and developers charges more than a freelancer</li><li><strong>Content and copywriting:</strong> some quotes include professional content writing, others expect you to provide it</li></ul><p>A quote of Rs. 10,000 and a quote of Rs. 1,00,000 are rarely delivering the same thing. The cheaper quote is usually a template installed with your logo and text. The expensive one is a designed, tested, and optimised experience built around your business goals.</p>
```

### Section 2
- **Section Link Title**: Website Design Pricing Models in India
- **Title**: Website Design Pricing Models in India
- **Description**:
```
<p>Web design agencies in India price their work in three main ways:</p><h4>1. Fixed-Price / Package Model (Most Common)</h4><p>You pay a one-time fee for a complete website: design, development, testing, and launch. This is the most common model for small business and corporate websites.</p><ul><li><strong>Price range:</strong> Rs. 15,000 to Rs. 5,00,000+ depending on scope</li><li><strong>Best for:</strong> businesses that know exactly what they need and want a predictable one-time cost</li><li><strong>Keep in mind:</strong> "fixed" usually covers the agreed scope only. Extra pages or features are charged separately.</li></ul><h4>2. Hourly Model</h4><p>You pay for the actual design and development time used.</p><ul><li><strong>Price range:</strong> Rs. 800 to Rs. 5,000+ per hour depending on the seniority of the team</li><li><strong>Best for:</strong> ongoing work, small tweaks, or projects where the scope is not clearly defined</li><li><strong>Keep in mind:</strong> cost can be unpredictable if the project scope grows</li></ul><h4>3. Monthly / Subscription Model</h4><p>You pay a monthly fee that covers design, development, hosting, maintenance, and sometimes content updates.</p><ul><li><strong>Price range:</strong> Rs. 5,000 to Rs. 50,000+ per month</li><li><strong>Best for:</strong> businesses that want the website continuously updated without a large upfront payment</li><li><strong>Keep in mind:</strong> over a few years the total cost usually exceeds a one-time build</li></ul>
```

### Section 4
- **Section Link Title**: What Should Be Included in a Website Design Quote?
- **Title**: What Should Be Included in a Website Design Quote?
- **Description**:
```
<p>A complete website design quote in 2026 should include all of the following:</p><h4>UI/UX Design</h4><p>Wireframes, mobile-first responsive design, a design system, and a prototype you can review before development begins. This is where most of the perceived quality of your website is decided.</p><h4>Development</h4><p>Front-end and back-end development, including the CMS (WordPress, custom PHP, React, or similar), page templates, and any integrations such as payment gateways, CRMs, or analytics.</p><h4>Content Setup</h4><p>Migration of your text, images, and media into the site. Some agencies include professional copywriting in the quote; others charge extra, so always confirm.</p><h4>Basic SEO Setup</h4><p>Meta titles and descriptions, URL structure, XML sitemap, schema markup, image optimisation, and mobile speed optimisation. A website that is not search-friendly from day one costs more to fix later.</p><h4>Security and Hosting Setup</h4><p>SSL certificate, secure hosting configuration, and admin backup setup. Ongoing hosting and maintenance are usually billed separately.</p><h4>Training and Support</h4><p>Training on how to update your own website and a defined post-launch support window (typically 30 to 90 days) for bug fixes.</p>
```

### Section 5
- **Section Link Title**: Hidden Costs That Push Website Prices Up
- **Title**: Hidden Costs That Push Website Prices Up
- **Description**:
```
<p>The quoted design fee is only part of the total cost of owning a website. Budget for these additional items:</p><ul><li><strong>Domain name:</strong> Rs. 800 – Rs. 1,500 per year for a standard .in or .com domain</li><li><strong>Hosting:</strong> Rs. 5,000 – Rs. 50,000+ per year depending on traffic and performance needs</li><li><strong>SSL certificate:</strong> often included with hosting, but sometimes charged separately</li><li><strong>Content and images:</strong> professional photography, custom graphics, and stock photos add Rs. 5,000 – Rs. 1,00,000</li><li><strong>Ongoing maintenance:</strong> Rs. 2,000 – Rs. 15,000+ per month for updates, backups, and security</li><li><strong>SEO and digital marketing:</strong> a beautiful website generates nothing by itself; marketing is a separate budget line</li></ul><p>As a rule of thumb, plan for the design and development cost to be 60–70% of your total first-year website budget, with the rest going to domain, hosting, content, and setup.</p>
```

### Section 6
- **Section Link Title**: Red Flags in Cheap Website Design Quotes
- **Title**: Red Flags in Cheap Website Design Quotes
- **Description**:
```
<p>Low prices are attractive, but these warning signs usually mean the cheap quote will cost you more later:</p><ul><li><strong>"Free website design with hosting":</strong> the cost is hidden in long-term hosting contracts or ad credits, and you rarely own the design.</li><li><strong>Rs. 5,000 to Rs. 10,000 "complete websites":</strong> these are template installs with your logo dropped in. They are not custom-designed and cannot be extended easily.</li><li><strong>No prototype or design review:</strong> if the agency will not show you a design before development, the result rarely matches your expectations.</li><li><strong>No mention of SEO or mobile optimisation:</strong> in 2026, mobile-first indexing means a non-mobile-optimised site is effectively invisible on Google.</li><li><strong>You do not own the code:</strong> confirm that the finished website and its code belong to you, not the agency.</li><li><strong>No post-launch support:</strong> if bugs appear after a week, will anyone fix them?</li></ul><p>Ask for a clear, itemised scope: number of pages, features, revisions, content inclusion, SEO setup, training, and support window. An agency that can document this is far more likely to deliver.</p>
```

### Section 7
- **Section Link Title**: How to Choose the Right Website Design Investment
- **Title**: How to Choose the Right Website Design Investment
- **Description**:
```
<ol><li>Start with the goal of the website. Is it to generate leads, sell products, build credibility, or show a portfolio? The goal decides the features, and the features decide the price.</li><li>Compare at least three itemised quotes. Compare scope per rupee, not just the headline price.</li><li>Look at the agency's portfolio and case studies. A design that works for a clinic will not necessarily work for a D2C brand.</li><li>Ask about timelines, revision limits, and what happens after launch.</li><li>Check whether the quote includes basic SEO, mobile optimisation, and analytics setup. These are no longer optional.</li><li>Think of the website as an investment, not an expense. A well-built site that converts visitors into customers easily pays for itself in the first few months.</li></ol>
```

## FAQs (add via "Add FAQ")

1. **Question**: How much does a website design cost in India in 2026?
   **Answer**:
   ```
   <p>A professional website design in India in 2026 costs between Rs. 15,000 and Rs. 5,00,000+. A small business website of 5–10 pages costs Rs. 15,000–Rs. 50,000, a corporate website Rs. 50,000–Rs. 1,50,000, and an e-commerce store Rs. 60,000–Rs. 3,00,000.</p>
   ```

2. **Question**: Why are website design quotes so different?
   **Answer**:
   ```
   <p>Because "website design" covers everything from template-based brochure sites to custom e-commerce platforms. The price difference reflects the number of pages, feature complexity, custom UI/UX design, team experience, and whether SEO setup, content, and post-launch support are included in the quote.</p>
   ```

3. **Question**: Is a cheap website a bad investment?
   **Answer**:
   ```
   <p>Not always, but very cheap quotes (Rs. 5,000–Rs. 10,000) are usually template installs with no custom design, weak SEO, and no support. If the website is the first impression your business makes, a poor design can cost you more in lost credibility than the money you saved.</p>
   ```

4. **Question**: What extra costs come with building a website?
   **Answer**:
   ```
   <p>Beyond the design fee, budget for the domain (Rs. 800–Rs. 1,500/year), hosting (Rs. 5,000–Rs. 50,000+/year), content and images (Rs. 5,000–Rs. 1,00,000), and ongoing maintenance (Rs. 2,000–Rs. 15,000/month). Expect design and development to be roughly 60–70% of your first-year website budget.</p>
   ```
